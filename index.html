Step 2

Clean it up

Honest note: "Reduce background noise" removes steady noise (hiss, hum, fan, AC) by learning the room tone from your quietest moments and subtracting it - it will not remove one-off sounds like a door slam or a bark. Trimming removes silence and pauses. Neither removes spoken filler words ("um", "ah") yet - that still needs a speech-to-text pass, a planned next step.
Folding

How foldable is this recording?

A preview of what TetraCubeDB will measure on the VPS: fold the audio into its strongest frequency coefficients (the rule) and see how much you keep versus how much you can drop. Honest both ways - tonal voice folds well, noisy audio barely folds. Listen to hear the tradeoff.

Add your music

Step 4

Export & publish

Audio file - for Substack & podcast hosts
Video file - for YouTube, TikTok, Facebook

Those platforms need video, not audio. This renders your episode as a video (your cover image plus an animated waveform) while the audio plays. It records in real time, so a 5-minute episode takes about 5 minutes.

Open the uploader

These open each platform's upload page in a new tab. Download the right file above first, then drop it in.

Honest note - why this isn't one-click auto-posting: publishing straight into these platforms isn't something a private browser app can do safely. Each needs a logged-in app approval and secret keys kept on a server (not in your browser), and Substack has no API to publish a podcast at all. The video sites also require video, which is why the step above exists. So Manifold Podcast does the part it can do well: make the exact file each platform wants and take you to its uploader. Fully automated posting would be a separate per-platform server project - say the word if you ever want to go there.
