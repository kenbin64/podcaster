🎬 Director

One button. The Director loads your script, divides it into scenes (a few lines each), runs your calibration, then rolls a take of each scene the moment your delivery is in the zone. It only asks you to do something when it needs you (turn on the camera, pick the episode). A scene is a series of takes; a take is one recording.
Say action to roll, cut to stop, slow down / pick up the pace to nudge the prompter. Or type a change above: exact edits are instant; natural rewrites use your local model (Ollama), nothing leaves the machine.
▸ Manual controls (the Director does these for you)
One scene = one whole point (a few lines). Each {section} starts a new scene; drop {take} to split a long point. Each scene can hold several takes (recordings); the starred take is the keeper. Assemble stitches the chosen takes with a soft dissolve at each seam.

Video tools

Editing the clip on the timeline above. Scrub to a frame, Split, then Delete or Move the pieces. Click a piece on the Video track to select it.

Voiceover & narration (local, free)

Turn any text into a spoken voiceover. Preview works right now in your browser. Generate file uses a local open-source voice (Piper) so nothing leaves your machine; if it is not installed yet it tells you how. Use it for narration over video, or for voiceover-only episodes (no camera).

AI image (local, free)

Make a graphic from a prompt with a local open-source image model (Stable Diffusion via Automatic1111 or Forge on port 7860). Private, on your machine. If it is not running it tells you.

AI video (local, frontier)

Animate an image (image-to-video) or make a clip from text (text-to-video) with a local open-source model (LTX-Video via ComfyUI). This is the heavy frontier: needs a strong GPU, clips are short, quality varies. Free and private. Pick a look, same styles as images.

Score: manifold-plucked, piano + techy pulse (live, free, synth)

A soft piano score that holds a theme and shifts with your script. The melody notes are read off the manifold (z = x·y) and quantized to the mood's scale, then struck like a piano string. No stored music: the geometry itself is what gets plucked. All synth, no files, no licenses.
Script cues: {music | warm} sets the mood, {theme | Ken} gives a character/place/event its own recurring motif, {music | off} stops. Intensity also swells at each section.

Camera & mic

Live: record yourself now with the teleprompter. Prerecorded: load a clip you already shot and the app overlays the intro, graphics, transitions, and end scene, then re-records it. Set the Pace so the cues track the clip, and nudge any timing in the Editor.
▸ Use your phone as the camera (USB)
1. Connect the phone with a USB cable.

3. Click ↻ Detect cameras, then pick the phone in the Camera list (it shows as "Android", "USB", or "Iriun").
4. Click Enable camera.

Teleprompter

Brief: the point + the assets to include (optional, makes the AI script richer)
AI writes the whole timeline script from your brief (what to include, the point, the assets, all placed on the timeline) to the built-in directive; Hybrid weaves your notes in; or write/Import your own (Human) and edit anytime. AI and Hybrid use your private local model.
The line glides so the word you are speaking stays dead-center under the camera, with the next words visible so your phrasing stays natural. Lower the word size to see more words at once. Record auto-starts it after the countdown. Space pauses or resumes.
▸ Bracket directives (smart prompter cues, never recorded, never spoken)
Anything in [ brackets ] is a cue for you, stripped from the line you read.
[to camera] or [eyes up] — the lens label flashes ● EYES TO CAMERA ● and the dot glows, so you lift to the lens for that beat.
[pause] or [pause 1.5] — holds the word so you can breathe.
[slower] / [faster] — change the pace.
[warm] / [firm] / [serious] / [excited] / [curious] — set the tone (the coach face mirrors it).
[intensity up] / [softer] — lift or ease the energy.

Editor — add / remove elements

Every graphic, caption, pause, transition, and cue in your show is listed here. Remove one, or add a new one at any word, after the fact. Changes update the script, the teleprompter, and the timeline live.
AI pass — what changed
Auto-edit places eye-contact cues at the open, the closing thought, and the sign-off, and tidies duplicate clears. Transitions between sections drops a "next topic" page before each section header so the video flows with no jarring cuts. ⦿ uses the word currently under the lens as the spot to insert.

Asset Studio — make on-brand graphics

Build a graphic in the show's style, no drawing app needed. It is added to your media instantly (use it in a cue or the editor), and you can download the SVG into your project's assets folder.

Delivery coach (script-driven)

As the prompter scrolls, the face reacts to that line of your script — nods at sentence ends, brow-up on questions, smiles on upbeat lines, an emphasis bob on CAPS or *marked* words. Mirror it peripherally for a natural, content-matched delivery while you look into the lens.

Intro music (manifold — z = x·y, no files)

Synthesized live from the manifold (z = x·y) — no sound files. It plays from the top of the take, holds, then fades. Come in talking just as it starts to fade. Or hit "Fade music now" to fade on your cue. The mixed music + voice is captured into the recording.
